About HER XV

HER XV IS MORE THAN APPAREL — IT'S A MOVEMENT FOR WOMEN WHO PLAY BOLD AND LIVE STRONGER.

HER XV was born from a love of rugby and passion to create a space for women in the game. We believe rugby isn't just a sport — it's a community, a culture and a powerful platform for women to be seen, celebrated, and supported.

Every piece we create carries the spirit of the game and the heart of the women who play it. This is more than clothing — it's a movement.
